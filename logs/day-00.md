- Focus on building foundation, not chasing impossible metrics
- Treat failure as narrative data, not project death

### Risk: Insufficient visibility
**Likelihood:** High (no existing audience)  
**Impact:** No service requests  
**Mitigation:**
- Post publicly on dial.wtf

- Offer genuinely useful free services
- Let work speak louder than marketing

### Risk: Scope creep
**Likelihood:** Medium (enthusiasm exceeds capacity)  
**Impact:** Quality degradation  
**Mitigation:**
- Maintain hard constraints
- Human approval gate prevents overcommitment
- Focus on 2-3 core services initially
